Scanner

Every mover, the moment it moves.

Persistent IBKR scanner rosters for Gappers, Gainers, Losers, After Hours and Large Cap. Each row carries what you need before you click: gap, relative volume, float, short interest, news and halts.

  • A name is on the board before its first tick arrives
  • One-click filters for gap, float, relative volume, news and halts
  • Catalysts classified from SEC filings and press wires, not guessed
  • A watch list that taps you on the shoulder when a name moves

HOD Momo

Know the second a stock makes a new high.

HOD Momo watches every name on your desk and fires when one breaks its high of day with the volume to mean it. A master gate keeps untradeable names off the board, and bursts fold into one alert instead of a wall of noise.

Thirteen strategies

Each with its own rules and sound, from five-minute surges to low-float runners.

The numbers on the alert

Change, relative volume, float, gap, volume and momentum, right where the alert fires.

Alerts off the glass

Discord, Telegram and webhook alerts, plus toasts for the names on your watch list.

Trader View

Four timeframes. One glance. Zero guesswork.

Ten-second, one-minute, five-minute and daily charts side by side, with VWAP, EMAs, MACD, session shading and drawing tools. The day's support and resistance are drawn where they matter, so you never trade into a wall you didn't see.

Store-first charts

Bars paint from the local store instantly; IBKR history fills in behind, never blocking a click.

Levels that matter

High of day, premarket high, VWAP, round numbers and tested tops, measured before they were drawn.

Hotkeys for speed

Bind any Nova action to a key, or import your DAS Trader hotkeys in one step.

Level 2 & Time & Sales

See what the book is hiding.

A depth ladder and tape built for speed. Nova watches the book for you: size that was pulled versus size that actually traded, hidden sellers soaking up buyers, and every print colored by where it hit.

  • Pulled and traded size marked right on the ladder
  • Hidden buyers and sellers outlined where they hold
  • Your entry, stop and target shown inside the book

The plan

A plan before every trade.

Nova reads the stock with you: the setup forming on the chart, an entry, a stop and a 2:1 target sized to the risk you set, and every reason not to take it, in plain words. Then you choose who trades it.

  • Setups drawn on the chart as they form, fail or trigger
  • Seven signal tiles: in play, setups, momentum, tape, short, float, halts
  • You, Nova with your approval, or Nova alone in practice

Safety

Safety is the default, not a setting.

Every order, from your clicks, your hotkeys or Nova's bots, passes through one door with the same checks. Live trading is locked behind your PIN, and getting flat is never blocked.

Local-first

No cloud. No subscription. No middleman.

The engine binds to 127.0.0.1. Market data comes straight from your own IB Gateway, and your recordings, ledgers and logs stay on your drive.

Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Open an issue.

Is Nova free?

Yes. Nova is open source under the MIT license. There is no subscription and no hosted service to pay for.

Which broker does it work with?

Interactive Brokers, through IB Gateway on your machine. Market data and Live orders use your own IBKR account and data subscriptions.

Will Nova trade for me?

Only when you ask it to, and only in practice. Nova's bot trades the Paper and Sim venues. Live orders stay yours: unattended live trading is rejected in code, and arming Live needs your PIN.

Do I need IB Gateway to try it?

No. The live demo runs the whole desk in your browser on sample data, with no broker, no keys and no backend.

Which platforms does it run on?

Windows is the supported desktop, with an installer that updates itself. The engine is Python and the desk is a web app, so you can also run it from source in a browser.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The API binds to 127.0.0.1, and recordings, ledgers and logs stay on your drive. Optional services such as news and earnings are read-only.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nova is software. You are responsible for your account, its permissions and every order you send.
